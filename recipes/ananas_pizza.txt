Ananas Pizza
==============

A hawaiian pizza for any funny occasion :)

- pineapple
- ananas
- a lot of cheese
- flour

Ingredients:
- 1 pizza dough (store-bought or homemade)
- 1/2 cup tomato sauce
- 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
- 1 cup pineapple chunks (fresh or canned, drained)
- 1/2 cup cooked ham, diced (optional)
- Olive oil
- Salt and pepper

Instructions:
1. Preheat oven to 475°F (245°C). If using a pizza stone, place it in the oven to heat.
2. Roll out the pizza dough on a floured surface to about 12 inches in diameter.
3. Transfer the dough to a pizza peel or baking sheet dusted with flour or cornmeal.
4. Brush the dough lightly with olive oil.
5. Spread the tomato sauce evenly over the dough, leaving a small border for the crust.
6. Sprinkle half of the mozzarella cheese over the sauce.
7. Add the pineapple chunks and ham (if using) evenly over the cheese.
8. Top with the remaining mozzarella cheese.
9. Season with salt and pepper if desired.
10. Bake for 12-15 minutes, or until the crust is golden and cheese is bubbly and slightly browned.
11. Remove from oven and let cool for a few minutes before slicing.

Tips:
- For a crispier crust, bake on a preheated pizza stone.
- Drain canned pineapple well to avoid a soggy pizza.
- Adjust toppings to taste — some prefer extra cheese or no ham.
