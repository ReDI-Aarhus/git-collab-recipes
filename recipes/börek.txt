Börek

Ingredients:
- 1 package of phyllo dough
- 1 cup of feta cheese, crumbled
- 1 cup of spinach, chopped
- 1/2 cup of ricotta cheese
- 1/4 cup of chopped fresh parsley
- 1/4 cup of chopped fresh dill
- 1/4 cup of melted butter
- 1 egg, beaten



Instructions:
1. Preheat oven to 180 degrees C (350 degrees F).
2. In a bowl, combine feta cheese, spinach, ricotta cheese, parsley, and dill.
3. Lay one sheet of phyllo dough on a clean surface and brush with melted butter. Repeat with 3 more sheets, layering them on top of each other.
4. Spread the cheese and spinach mixture evenly over the phyllo dough, leaving a small border around the edges.
5. Roll the phyllo dough tightly into a log, starting from one of the long sides. Brush the outside of the log with beaten egg.
6. Place the log on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper and bake for 25-30 minutes, or until golden brown and crispy.
7. Remove from oven and let cool for a few minutes before slicing and serving.
