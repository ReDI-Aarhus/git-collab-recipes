Guacamole
=========

Fresh and simple — best made just before serving.

Ingredients:
- 3 ripe avocados
- 1 lime, juiced
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 white onion, finely diced
- 2 Roma tomatoes, seeded and diced
- 2 tablespoons fresh cilantro, chopped
- 1 small jalapeno, minced (seeds removed for less heat)
- 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder (optional)

Instructions:
1. Halve and pit the avocados, scoop flesh into a bowl
2. Mash with a fork to your preferred consistency (chunky or smooth)
3. Add lime juice and salt, stir to combine
4. Fold in onion, tomato, cilantro, and jalapeno
5. Taste and adjust salt and lime juice
6. Serve immediately or press plastic wrap directly onto the surface

Tips:
- An avocado is ripe when it yields to gentle pressure but is not mushy
- The lime juice slows browning — do not skip it
- For extra heat, keep some jalapeno seeds in
- Press plastic wrap onto the surface to slow browning when storing
