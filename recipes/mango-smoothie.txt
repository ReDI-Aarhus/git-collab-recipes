Mango Smoothie
==============

A quick tropical smoothie for any time of day.

Serves: 2
Prep time: 5 minutes

Ingredients:
- 2 ripe mangoes, peeled and cubed (or 2 cups frozen mango)
- 1 cup plain yogurt
- 1/2 cup orange juice
- 1 tablespoon honey (optional)
- 1/2 cup ice cubes

Instructions:
1. Add all ingredients to a blender
2. Blend on high until smooth and creamy
3. Taste and add honey if desired
4. Pour into glasses and serve immediately

Tips:
- Frozen mango gives a thicker, colder smoothie without extra ice
- Add a pinch of chili powder for a tropical twist
- For a dairy-free version, use coconut milk instead of yogurt
