Ingredients:
- 400g flour
- 200g pepperoni
- 4 large egg yolks
- 100g Pecorino Romano cheese, finely grated
- Freshly ground black pepper
- Salt for pasta water

Instructions:
1. Make a pizza dough by mixing flour, water, oil, egg yolks
2. Knead it well and mave a flat surface 
3. Add tomato sauce , then cheese and pepperoni
4. Place it in Pizza oven and heat for 200C for 20-25 mins
