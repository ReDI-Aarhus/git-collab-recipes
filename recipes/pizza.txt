Ingredients:

Pizza dough
Pizza sauce
1–2 cups shredded mozzarella
Toppings 

Instructions:

Preheat oven: 220–250 °C (425–480 °F).
Shape dough: Divide into 2 balls and roll out.
Add sauce: Spread a thin layer.
Add cheese + toppings.
Bake: 10–15 minutes until crust is golden and cheese bubbly.

Tips:

Preheat a baking tray or pizza stone for a crispier crust.
Brush crust edges with olive oil for better color.
Add fresh basil after baking for flavor
